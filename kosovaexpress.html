Rooted in agriculture, driven by reliability.

Our Portfolio

Moving agriculture forward.

From harvest to market, Kosova Express LLC transports agricultural goods safely and on time. We handle grains, produce, livestock feed, and fresh crops with the care, temperature control, and reliability your business depends on.

What We Haul

Grains & Cereals

Bulk transport of wheat, corn, soybeans, and barley in dry hopper trailers — sealed, weighed, and delivered with moisture and quality intact.

Fresh Produce

Temperature-controlled reefers keep fruits and vegetables fresh from farm to distribution center. Real-time monitoring ensures ideal conditions throughout the trip.

Feed & Hay

Reliable delivery of hay bales, livestock feed, and supplements to farms and ranches — with flexible scheduling that fits your feeding cycle.

Farm Equipment

Flatbed and step-deck transport for tractors, harvesters, and implements. Load secured with industry-grade strapping and full cargo insurance.
